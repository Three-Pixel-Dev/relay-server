# APPENDIX 1 — PROJECT INFORMATION AND KEY FUNCTIONS

## A. Project information

- **Project name:** Relay (subject to change later)
- **Type:** Web application (desktop and mobile browser), English, international market
- **Summary:** Relay is a digital product assistant. A user gives it what they know, their documents, or a creator's YouTube or Instagram link. Relay reads the sources, finds a profitable angle, writes and designs a selling-ready PDF product, sells it through Whop, runs the channels that bring buyers, and makes promo videos. Relay also finds creators who have an email list, sends them a partnership offer, and builds the product for those who accept.
- **Business model:** Subscription, $19 per month or $149 per year, 7-day free trial. Users connect their own AI provider keys. Relay takes no cut of sales.
- **User roles:** Subscriber, Admin
- **Reference materials:** Product Requirements Document, clickable prototype (fresh account), clickable prototype (demo account), technical handoff notes, prototype source code. The prototype defines screens and behaviour. Production is a new build.
- **Payment provider for subscriptions:** to be confirmed by the Client before billing work starts.

## B. Key functions

1. **Accounts and subscription:** sign up, login, no trial, 6 months and yearly plans, billing management.
2. **Start screen:** "What do you know, care about, or want to help people with?", Guided or Fast mode, trending ideas, add sources, build for someone else.
3. **Sources:** upload documents (PDF, Word, text, transcripts, audio, video) or paste YouTube, Instagram or web links. Relay indexes every passage per product.
4. **Source summary:** niche, transformation, content themes with evidence level, verdict, audience and voice. User approves or changes it before anything is written.
5. **Build for a creator:** paste a creator's YouTube or Instagram link, get a creator overview, and build the product in their voice.
6. **Profitable pockets:** four "I help X do Y without Z" options with scores and the evidence behind each score.
7. **Product type and structure:** eBook, workbook, planner, checklist, template pack or mini-course, with an editable table of contents.
8. **Manuscript:** written section by section from the sources, with citations per section and an approval stop after the first section.
9. **Branding and files:** palettes, type, covers, and export to PDF, EPUB, DOCX, mockups and an editable design file. Files are always downloadable.
10. **Selling on Whop:** connect Whop, create the product, checkout link, storefront entry and affiliate programme, and sync sales back to Relay. Etsy, Gumroad, Payhip and Amazon KDP are optional extras.
11. **Partners, find:** search a niche on YouTube and Instagram for creators with a public email-list mention and business email, or import the user's own list by paste or CSV.
12. **Partners, outreach:** one email and two follow-ups sent from the user's own mailbox, editable template, revenue split offer, confirmation before sending, daily limit, stop on reply, unsubscribe handling.
13. **Partners, replies and selling together:** reply sorting (interested or not), one-click "Build their product", partner revenue share set on Whop, both shares shown on the product page.
14. **Distribute and Promote:** channel cards (marketplace search, Pinterest, lead magnet and email, affiliates, small paid test) with weekly approval, and ten short promo videos with an approval stop after the first.
15. **Agent thread, connections and admin:** Proceed / Suggest / Cancel at every step with an AI spend meter, a Connections page for all linked accounts and AI keys, My Products with sales and revenue, and an admin dashboard.

## C. Delivery phases

- **Phase 1:** Functions 1 to 10 (accounts, sources, build, files, Whop).
- **Phase 2:** Functions 11 to 13 (Partners).
- **Phase 3:** Function 14, Distribute.
- **Phase 4:** Function 14, Promote, and the optional selling platforms in function 10.
- **Note:** Function 15 is delivered across all phases as each part is built.
